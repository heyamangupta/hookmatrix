Questions.

Will it guarantee better results?

No. It gives you more distinct angles to test. Nobody can reliably predict which hook wins, so the point is testing five, not betting on one.

Does the policy flag guarantee approval?

No. It marks claims worth a second look before you launch. Platform policies change, and enforcement is theirs.

What do I get out?

For each hook: text, camera framing, B-roll cue, overlay text and a risk flag. Export as CSV.

How are URLs handled?

URLs are read automatically via Jina to parse the product offer and structure hooks.
